In this message, Saju describes the need to be in the light of God. The Spirit of the Lord was moving over the earth that was void and shapeless. It was dark; but God pronounced, “Let there be Light” and it was light. When your life becomes void and shapeless, ask the Holy Spirit to move over your darkness and you can expect the miracle; You will be in Light.
