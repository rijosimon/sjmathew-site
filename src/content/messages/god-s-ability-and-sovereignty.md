Most of the time we hear preachers asking us to believe in the ability of God. However believing in the ability is not a unique faith. Faith is trust. When we trust in God, we are not only proclaiming the ability of God but also acknowledge the sovereignty of God and  when you trust, you will prove you trust through obedience.
