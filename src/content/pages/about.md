---
title: "About Saju"
---

Saju John Mathew (known as “Saju”) is a servant of God who wants to be “obedient to the heavenly call.” At thirteen, as a schoolchild, he came to know his Lord and Savior Jesus. Along with his friends, he began witnessing about Jesus in school and in the streets.

In the twelfth grade, God asked him to start a journal in the Malayalam language. It was called “Maruppacha,” which means “Oasis,” and it became one of the most widely read journals among the born-again community of his province.

As a university student, he laboured in the conception, beginning and growth of the Inter Collegiate Prayer Fellowship, a fellowship of students from full-gospel churches.

After his journalism studies, he concentrated on writing books and teaching the Bible. For some time he was part of the ship *Doulos* of Operation Mobilisation.

In 1994, God asked him to start a pioneer mission movement in North India, and that was the beginning of Jesus Mission. Yet God wanted him to continue as a Bible teacher. He has inspired many to dwell in the living word of God, across the Indian subcontinent and in Africa.

This website aims to showcase his many works, recorded messages and e-books, freely, so that they may be a blessing to many.

Saju still works with the missionary organization Jesus Mission India, which has done a great deal in touching the lives of many people in the remote villages of North India.
