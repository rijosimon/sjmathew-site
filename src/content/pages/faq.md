## 1. How can I play the files in the website?

You can press the play button below the title of the message.

If it is a video file, click anywhere in the preview image or the button highlighted in the image below.

## 2. Is it possible for me to download the audio and video files?

You can download it for sure. We have placed various links for downloading files. For the new messages, we even provide links with different sizes so that users with slow connection can also take advantage of that.

## 3. I have downloaded the video/audio, but cannot play it.

We compress the files with winzip for various reasons. Please check whether you’ve unzipped the file before trying to play it.

If your computer has winzip/winrar installed in it, do the following steps

Step 1: Right click the downloaded file
Step 2: Select Extract Here or Extract to “Folder name”

If your computer does not have any compressing software like winrar installed in it, do the following steps
Step 1: Right click the downloaded file
Step 2: Select Extract All
Step 3: Click Extract button

## 4. My download stops during the middle / I cannot download the file

All links in the page are tested before publishing. Please try from a different browser/try re-installing the browser. If your connection is slow, you may also try downloading the files with smaller size or the sessions individually.

## 5. Is there any way to get an alert when a new message is posted in the site?

Sure. You can subscribe to our website to get future alerts.

**Step1:** Enter your email-id and click the “Subscribe” button

**Step2:** Enter the text in the highlighted area in the text box below. (Please note that the text may be different in your case). Click the “Complete subscription Request” button.

**Step3:** Please check your inbox for a verification message from “FeedBurner Email Subscriptions”, the service that delivers email subscriptions for the website. You will need to click a link listed in this message to activate your subscription. If you don’t see a confirmation e-mail in a reasonable amount of time please check your bulk/spam folder.
